⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first meditation, with Lucia on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1 (after the mission Down to Earth), is gone for good after Chapter 1. Do it in Chapter 1 to be safe — it is still unconfirmed whether it is required. Every other trophy can be earned after the story.

Roadmap to the platinum

Built from PowerPyx's roadmap (difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, 0 online trophies). The story is 5 chapters and takes 60–80 hours; there is no mission replay, no mission medals and no difficulty setting — so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and before the final mission.

  1. Step 1 — Finish the story 60–80 hours

    Play the story and enjoy it. Take optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles and study animals as you go — progress made during the story already counts toward the 100% checklist, even though the checklist only appears after the credits. The only thing to watch for is the Chapter 1 meditation above.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist + 70 of 80 challenges about 100 hours

    After the credits, the 35-task 100% checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is roughly 60% true completion: most side quests are not needed, collectibles are only partly needed, and any 70 of the 80 challenges will do. This step earns A Long Road, the hardest trophy, and most of the collectible and activity trophies along the way.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count) and the character questlines, then mop up whatever is left — the four UNVERIFIED trophies are still unconfirmed, so check back for them. The platinum pops when the last trophy does.

Keep cheats off. Cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx / GTABase), so a trophy run with cheats on earns nothing. Cheats only unlock after the story in any case.
